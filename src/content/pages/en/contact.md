## Address

**Ixelles Hockey Club**
Rue Volta 18, 1050 Ixelles

[Open in Maps](https://www.openstreetmap.org/search?query=Rue%20Volta%2018%2C%201050%20Ixelles)

## Getting here

There are two ways in:

- **from the top**, through the sports centre: Rue Volta 18, 1050 Ixelles;
- **from the bottom**, through the pétanque ground: Rue de Brebis 77, 1050 Ixelles.

Parking on Rue de Brebis is a blue zone: use a parking disc, and watch the time limit and the parking charges on Saturdays.

> To complete: tram and bus stops, bike racks.
