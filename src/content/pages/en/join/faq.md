## What kit do I need?

A stick the right size, shin guards, a mouthguard and the club kit for matches, from our partner [Hockey-Player](https://hockeyplayer.shop/). The full list is on [Getting started](/en/join/getting-started/#kit).

## When are matches?

At weekends, from 1 September to 20 November and from 1 March to 20 May, with a winter break in between. Each team's fixtures are in the [Hockey Belgium app](/membres/formalites/#app-hockey-belgium).

## Do you play in the rain?

Yes. We only stop if the pitch is flooded or the forecast is 3 °C or less. The pitch status is shown at the top of every page.

## What does volunteering mean?

The club runs on volunteers: parent-managers, umpires, coaches, event organisers. Everyone gives a little time in the way that suits them. See [Volunteering](/membres/benevolat/) (in French).
