Joining Ixelles Hockey Club means accepting the club's [terms and conditions](/membres/formalites/#conditions-generales) (in French) and its [charter](/club/a-propos/#charte).

## 2026-27 outdoor season

| Category | Fee |
| --- | --- |
| U6 | 320 € |
| U7 | 350 € |
| U8 | 350 € |
| U9 | 370 € |
| U10 to U12 | 380 € |
| U14, U16 and U19 | 440 € |
| Seniors and Masters | 440 € |
| Gents and Ladies | 390 € |

Fees for members of the same household are capped at **€1,320** in total, and a €50 reduction per member applies from the third member.

### Surcharges

- Double-competition affiliation (Seniors + Gents/Ladies): €100.
- Payment after 30 September: €100.

### Leaving the club

- Before 31 July: free.
- Before the official start of the season: €100.
- After the official start of the season: the full fee is due.

The fee must be paid in full by 30 September. Full details are in the [terms and conditions](/media/conditions-generales-2026-2027.pdf) (in French).

## Paying in instalments

You can ask to spread this season's fee over several payments. Send the [instalment request form](https://app.twizzit.com/v2/public/form/1edce197be55c7182392de0fe92c0f7b) **before your invoice is due**. To be granted a plan, you must pay at least €100.

## Health insurance refunds

Some Belgian health insurers (mutuelles) refund part of a sports club fee. See [mutuelle certificates](/membres/formalites/#mutuelle) (in French).
