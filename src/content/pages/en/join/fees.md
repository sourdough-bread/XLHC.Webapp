## Paying in instalments

You can ask to spread this season's fee over several payments. Send the [instalment request form](https://app.twizzit.com/v2/public/form/1edce197be55c7182392de0fe92c0f7b) **before your invoice is due**. Whatever plan you choose, at least €150 must be paid before 1 September, when the season starts.

## Health insurance refunds

Some Belgian health insurers (mutuelles) refund part of a sports club fee. See [mutuelle certificates](/membres/formalites/#mutuelle) (in French).
