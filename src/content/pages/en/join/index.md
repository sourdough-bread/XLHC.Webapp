Want to play hockey in Ixelles? Juniors from age 5, senior beginners or experienced players: there may well be a place for you.

1. **Discover** the club and come and try a session.
2. **Check** this season's fees.
3. **Sign up** and pay online through Twizzit.

## Places for 2026-27

Like many Brussels clubs, we are close to full.

**Juniors (age 5 to 18).** Some teams are already full with current members, so we can only take new children there as places free up. Others are more open. We are mainly looking for:

- girls born in 2011, 2012, 2013, 2018, 2019, 2020 and 2021;
- boys born in 2011, 2012, 2017, 2018, 2019, 2020 and 2021.

Even if your child's team is full, let us know: it helps us plan the squads.

**Seniors.** Senior teams are close to full too. On the men's side, the H5 team is recruiting; it plays in LFH FL3.

We pass every request on to the coach or captain concerned and reply as soon as we can.

## The XL-Card

Every member gets the XL-Card membership card when they join. It lets you use the pitch when it is free outside club activities, for example during school holidays, and gives a few perks at participating partners.
