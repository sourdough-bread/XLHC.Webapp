Field hockey is a team sport played with a stick and ball on an artificial pitch. New to the game? Here are a few tips.

Want to give it a go? The "Try a session" button opens a short form, and the coach for your age group replies with a date.

## Kit {#kit}

Every player, whatever their age, needs:

- a stick the right size;
- shin guards, with long socks to hold them in place;
- a mouthguard (in your mouth, not your pocket);
- suitable shoes (recommended, not required);
- sports clothes for training;
- the official club kit for matches, from our partner [Hockey-Player](https://hockeyplayer.shop/);
- a glove (optional);
- a water bottle and a bag for your things.

## How a season works

- The outdoor season runs from 1 September to the end of May, with a winter break from December to February.
- The first match is on the first weekend of September, and training starts the week before.
- The club belongs to the French-speaking hockey league (LFH) and follows the French-speaking school calendar.
- Matches are played from 1 September to 20 November (first round) and from 1 March to 20 May (second round).
- During the autumn, February and spring school holidays, teams usually play one weekend in three.
- Each team's fixtures are in the [Hockey Belgium app](/membres/formalites/#app-hockey-belgium).

## When do we play?

- As often as possible, even in the rain.
- Except when the pitch is flooded, or when the Belgian weather service (IRM) forecasts 3 °C or less.
- During the competition, teams train once or twice a week depending on age group; between rounds, once a week if the weather allows.
- The club takes two long breaks: 15 December to 15 January, and the summer holidays in July and August.

## Paperwork

- Only players who have paid their fee can take part in club activities, because of insurance.
- Membership renews automatically each season unless you [tell us you are leaving](/membres/formalites/#desaffiliation) before 30 June.
